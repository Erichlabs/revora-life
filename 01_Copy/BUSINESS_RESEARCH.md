## What is PEMF?

**Pulsed Electromagnetic Field (PEMF) therapy** is a non-invasive wellness technology that uses low-frequency electromagnetic pulses to stimulate cellular function and support the body's natural healing processes.

### Key Concepts

| Concept | Explanation |
|---------|-------------|
| **Electromagnetic Fields** | Natural energy fields that surround the Earth and all living things |
| **Pulsed Delivery** | Intermittent bursts rather than continuous exposure |
| **Low Frequency** | Typically 1-100 Hz, mimicking natural Earth frequencies (Schumann resonances) |
| **Cellular Stimulation** | Supports cellular membrane potential and ATP production |

### The Science Foundation

PEMF technology is based on decades of research into:
- Bioelectromagnetics
- Cellular biology
- NASA research on astronaut health
- European wellness applications
