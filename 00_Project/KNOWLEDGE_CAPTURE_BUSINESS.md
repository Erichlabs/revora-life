## Vision

**Statement:**
To become Australia's most trusted and premium destination for PEMF wellness technology, education, and solutions.

**Long-term:**
- National recognition in wellness technology
- Preferred partner for wellness professionals
- Industry leader in PEMF education
- Expansion into related wellness technologies

---

## Mission

**Statement:**
To make premium PEMF wellness technology accessible to Australians seeking natural, non-invasive wellness solutions through education, quality products, and expert support.

**Core Activities:**
1. Educate consumers about PEMF technology
2. Curate premium PEMF systems
3. Provide Australian-based support
4. Build trust through transparency

## Unique Selling Proposition (USP)

**Primary USP:**
Australia's premium PEMF destination — combining medical-grade professionalism with accessible education and local Australian support.

**Supporting Differentiators:**
- Education-first approach (not sales-heavy)

- Australian-based support and warranty

## Target Audience

### Primary: Health-Conscious Consumers
- **Age:** 35-65
- **Profile:** Invest in wellness proactively
- **Behavior:** Research-driven purchasing
- **Values:** Quality over price, natural solutions
- **Needs:** Recovery, sleep, energy, overall wellness

### Secondary: Athletes & Biohackers
- **Age:** 25-45
- **Profile:** Performance-focused
- **Behavior:** Early adopters, social media active
- **Values:** Cutting-edge technology, proven results
- **Needs:** Recovery, performance optimization

### Tertiary: Wellness Professionals
- **Profile:** Chiropractors, physiotherapists, naturopaths
- **Behavior:** Evidence-based decisions
- **Values:** Professional-grade equipment, patient results
- **Needs:** Clinical systems, professional support

## Customer Journey

### Awareness Stage
- Discovers PEMF through search, social, or referral
- Lands on educational content (What is PEMF?)

### Consideration Stage
- Explores product categories
- Compares options
- Reads testimonials
- Reviews FAQ

### Decision Stage
- Books consultation or contacts
- Receives personalized recommendation
- Makes purchase decision

### Post-Purchase
- Receives product and setup support
- Uses product with guidance
- Accesses ongoing support
- Becomes potential advocate
