## Target Audience

### Primary: Health-Conscious Consumers (60%)
- **Demographics:** Age 35-65, middle to upper income
- **Psychographics:** Proactive about health, research-driven
- **Pain Points:** Poor sleep, low energy, slow recovery, chronic discomfort
- **Goals:** Natural wellness solutions, better quality of life
- **Buying Behavior:** Values quality over price, seeks education

### Secondary: Athletes & Biohackers (25%)
- **Demographics:** Age 25-45, performance-focused
- **Psychographics:** Early adopters, data-driven, optimization-minded
- **Pain Points:** Recovery time, performance plateaus, injury prevention
- **Goals:** Competitive edge, faster recovery, peak performance
- **Buying Behavior:** Willing to invest in cutting-edge technology

### Tertiary: Wellness Professionals (15%)
- **Demographics:** Chiropractors, physiotherapists, naturopaths, clinic owners
- **Psychographics:** Evidence-based, patient outcome-focused
- **Pain Points:** Patient retention, treatment efficacy, differentiation
- **Goals:** Better patient results, practice growth, professional credibility
- **Buying Behavior:** Seeks clinical-grade equipment, professional support

## Customer Journey

### Stage 1: Awareness
**Touchpoints:** Google search, social media, referrals, content
**Actions:** Discovers PEMF, learns basics, identifies need
**Content:** "What is PEMF?", educational blog posts

### Stage 2: Consideration
**Touchpoints:** Website, email, consultation booking
**Actions:** Researches products, compares options, reads testimonials
**Content:** Benefits page, product comparisons, FAQ

### Stage 3: Decision
**Touchpoints:** Consultation, contact form, phone
**Actions:** Receives recommendation, evaluates offer, purchases
**Content:** Product pages, trust signals, guarantees

### Stage 4: Retention
**Touchpoints:** Support, email follow-up, community
**Actions:** Uses product, gets support, achieves results
**Content:** Usage guides, tips, success stories

### Stage 5: Advocacy
**Touchpoints:** Reviews, referrals, testimonials
**Actions:** Shares experience, recommends to others
**Content:** Testimonial requests, referral program

## Key Risks

### Market Risks
| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|------------|--------|------------|
| Low PEMF awareness | High | High | Education-first content strategy |
| Competitor price war | Medium | Medium | Premium positioning, value focus |
| Market saturation | Low | High | First-mover advantage in AU |

### Operational Risks
| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|------------|--------|------------|
