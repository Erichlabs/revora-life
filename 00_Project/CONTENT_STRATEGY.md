## Email Sequences

### Sequence 1: Lead Magnet Delivery (5 emails)
**Trigger:** Lead magnet download

1. **Immediate:** Deliver lead magnet + welcome
2. **Day 2:** "Did you know?" PEMF fact
3. **Day 4:** Educational content (related to magnet topic)
4. **Day 7:** Soft CTA (consultation booking)
5. **Day 14:** Final value + CTA

### Sequence 2: New Subscriber Welcome (3 emails)
**Trigger:** Newsletter signup

1. **Immediate:** Welcome + what to expect
2. **Day 3:** Best of content (top 3 posts)
3. **Day 7:** Invitation to follow on social

### Sequence 3: Post-Consultation (4 emails)
**Trigger:** Consultation completed

1. **Immediate:** Thank you + recap
2. **Day 2:** Product recommendation + quote
3. **Day 7:** FAQ addressing common objections
4. **Day 14:** Final follow-up + limited-time offer

### Sequence 4: Customer Onboarding (5 emails)
**Trigger:** Purchase completed

1. **Immediate:** Order confirmation + setup guide
2. **Day 3:** Unboxing and first use tips
3. **Day 7:** Best practices for results
4. **Day 14:** Success stories and inspiration
5. **Day 30:** Review request + referral program

### Sequence 5: Re-engagement (3 emails)
**Trigger:** 60 days no engagement

1. **Email 1:** "We miss you" + new content highlight
2. **Email 2:** Special offer or exclusive content
3. **Email 3:** Final attempt + unsubscribe option

## Content Governance

### Content Standards
- Australian English
- Medical claim compliance (no treatment claims)
- Evidence-based statements
- Clear, accessible language
- Professional tone

### Review Process
1. **Draft** — Writer creates content
2. **Review** — Editor checks quality, compliance
3. **Approve** — Final sign-off
4. **Publish** — Deploy to website
5. **Promote** — Share via channels

### Content Maintenance
- Quarterly content audits
- Annual pillar page updates
- Broken link checks (monthly)
- Outdated content refresh
