## Brand Positioning

**Position:**
Premium PEMF wellness technology brand with medical-grade credibility and Australian local support.

**Market Position:**
- Above: Mass-market wellness devices
- Below: Clinical-only medical equipment
- Different from: US/EU competitors without Australian presence

**Positioning Statement:**
"For health-conscious Australians seeking premium wellness technology, Revora Life is the only PEMF specialist that combines medical-grade quality with accessible education and local Australian support."

---

## Brand Personality

### Traits
1. **Professional** — Medical-grade, credible, trustworthy
2. **Premium** — High-quality, refined, sophisticated
3. **Educational** — Informative, transparent, helpful
4. **Approachable** — Warm, not clinical, Australian-friendly
5. **Innovative** — Forward-thinking, technology-focused

### Brand Voice
- Clear and accessible (not overly technical)
- Science-backed but not clinical
- Premium but not pretentious
- Warm but professional
- Australian English (not American)

### Tone Guidelines
| Context | Tone |
|---------|------|
| Educational content | Informative, helpful, clear |
| Product pages | Professional, benefit-focused |
| Testimonials | Warm, authentic, relatable |
| CTAs | Confident, inviting, action-oriented |
| Support | Friendly, patient, solution-focused |

## Typography System

### Font Families
- **Primary:** Inter (sans-serif) — Clean, modern, highly readable
- **Display:** Playfair Display (serif) — Elegant, premium, editorial

### Typography Philosophy
- Inter for UI and body (clarity, accessibility)
- Playfair for headlines (premium, sophisticated)
- Generous line height for readability
- Clear hierarchy through weight and size

### Scale
| Element | Font | Size | Weight |
|---------|------|------|--------|
| Hero H1 | Playfair | 48px | 700 |
| Page H1 | Playfair | 40px | 700 |
| Section H2 | Inter | 32px | 600 |
| Card H3 | Inter | 24px | 600 |
| Body | Inter | 16px | 400 |
| Small | Inter | 14px | 400 |
| Eyebrow | Inter | 14px | 600 |
