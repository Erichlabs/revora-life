## Accessibility

### Minimum Sizes
- Body text: 16px minimum
- Small text: 14px minimum (with sufficient contrast)
- Button text: 16px minimum

### Line Length
- Optimal: 50-75 characters per line
- Maximum: 90 characters per line

### Line Height
- Headings: 1.2 - 1.4
- Body: 1.6 - 1.8
