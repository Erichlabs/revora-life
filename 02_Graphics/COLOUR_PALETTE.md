## Usage Rules

### Backgrounds
- **Primary:** White (#FFFFFF)
- **Secondary:** Silver (#F5F7FA)
- **Dark:** Deep Blue (#0F2B52)
- **Accent:** Soft Green (#E6F4EA) — use sparingly

### Text
- **Headings:** Dark Grey (#2D3748) or Deep Blue (#0F2B52)
- **Body:** Dark Grey (#2D3748)
- **Secondary:** Medium Grey (#8A94A6)
- **On Dark:** White (#FFFFFF)

### CTAs
- **Primary:** Brand Blue (#1E5AAF)
- **Primary Hover:** Deep Blue (#0F2B52)
- **Secondary:** White with Brand Blue border
- **Tertiary:** Text link in Energy Blue (#4A90E2)

### Accents
- Use Wellness Green (#38A169) for:
  - Success messages
  - Checkmarks, positive indicators
  - Wellness-related highlights
- Use Energy Blue (#4A90E2) for:
  - Links
  - Interactive elements
  - Information highlights

## Accessibility

### Contrast Ratios
- Brand Blue on White: 5.2:1 ✅ (AA compliant)
- Dark Grey on White: 12.5:1 ✅ (AAA compliant)
- White on Deep Blue: 14.2:1 ✅ (AAA compliant)
- Medium Grey on White: 3.2:1 ⚠️ (Use for non-essential text only)

### Guidelines
- Always ensure 4.5:1 minimum contrast for body text
- Use darker shades for important text
- Test colour combinations for accessibility
