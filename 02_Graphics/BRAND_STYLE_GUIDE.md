# BRAND STYLE GUIDE
## Revora Life — Premium Wellness Brand

---

## Brand Positioning

### Brand Essence
**Cellular Wellness, Elevated**

### Brand Promise
Premium PEMF wellness technology, delivered with medical-grade professionalism and Australian support.

### Brand Personality
- **Professional** — Medical-grade credibility
- **Premium** — Luxury wellness aesthetic
- **Educational** — Transparent, informative
- **Trustworthy** — Science-backed, honest
- **Modern** — Contemporary, forward-thinking

### Brand Voice
- Clear and accessible
- Science-backed but not clinical
- Premium but not pretentious
- Warm but professional
- Australian-friendly

---

## Visual Philosophy

### Design Approach
**Swiss-Inspired Medical Wellness**

Clean precision meets wellness warmth. Medical credibility meets lifestyle appeal.

### Key Principles
1. **Clarity First** — Every element serves a purpose
2. **Breathing Room** — Generous white space
3. **Subtle Sophistication** — Refined details, not flash
4. **Trust Through Design** — Professional, credible aesthetic
5. **Wellness Warmth** — Inviting, not clinical

---

## Imagery Direction

### Photography Style
- Clean, bright, natural light
- Lifestyle wellness scenes
- People in moments of wellness (recovery, sleep, relaxation)
- Product photography: clean backgrounds, premium presentation
- Medical/professional settings for credibility

### Image Treatment
- Bright, clean exposure
- Natural colour grading
- Slight warmth (not cold/clinical)
- Sharp focus
- High resolution

### Subject Matter
- Wellness lifestyle
- Active recovery
- Restorative sleep
- Professional environments
- Product detail shots
- Abstract wellness concepts (cellular, energy)

---

## Iconography

### Style
- Line icons, 2px stroke
- Rounded corners
- Consistent 24x24px grid
- Minimal, clear metaphors

### Categories
- Wellness (heart, leaf, sun)
- Technology (signal, pulse, wave)
- Benefits (sleep, energy, recovery)
- Navigation (arrow, menu, close)
- Contact (phone, email, location)

---

## Animation Principles

### Philosophy
Subtle, purposeful, premium.

### Allowed
- Fade transitions (300ms ease)
- Subtle parallax on scroll
- Gentle hover states
- Loading skeletons

### Avoid
- Flashy effects
- Auto-playing video
- Excessive motion
- Distracting animations

---

## Application Guidelines

### Website
- Clean layouts
- Generous spacing
- Clear hierarchy
- Premium photography
- Subtle interactions

### Marketing
- Consistent colour palette
- Professional photography
- Clear messaging
- Premium paper stocks (print)

### Product
- Clean packaging
- Medical-grade presentation
- Clear instructions
- Premium unboxing
