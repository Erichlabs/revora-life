## Image Prompt

```
A professional medical disclaimer banner illustration for a wellness website. Horizontal format, low profile (wide and short aspect ratio approximately 4:1).

Design elements:
- Soft gradient background from light blue (#E8F4FC) to white, creating a clean medical feel
- Subtle medical cross or caduceus symbol on the left side, stylized and modern (not traditional red cross)
- Gentle wave or curved line pattern suggesting wellness and calm
- Small health-related icons scattered subtly: heart pulse line, wellness rings, or cellular patterns
- Professional, trustworthy aesthetic without being clinical or sterile
- Soft shadows and depth for modern look

Color palette:
- Primary: Soft blues (#1E5AAF light tint) and whites
- Accent: Subtle wellness green (#38A169) for small elements
- No harsh reds or alarming colors

Style: Clean, minimal, modern medical aesthetic. Premium wellness brand feel. Not cartoonish or overly clinical. Suitable for a high-end PEMF therapy website.

Text: NO TEXT on the image - text will be added via HTML overlay

Dimensions: Wide banner format, approximately 1200x300 pixels or 4:1 aspect ratio
Resolution: High quality for web, under 200KB optimized
```
