General Questions

What is PEMF therapy?

PEMF (Pulsed Electromagnetic Field) therapy uses low-frequency electromagnetic pulses to support cellular function and wellness. It's a non-invasive technology used by wellness seekers worldwide to support recovery, sleep, and overall vitality.

Is PEMF therapy safe?

PEMF therapy is generally considered safe for most people. The frequencies used are low-intensity and mimic natural Earth frequencies. However, always consult your healthcare provider before starting any new wellness practice, especially if you have medical conditions or implanted devices.

How long does a PEMF session take?

Session length varies by device and goal. Most sessions range from 10-30 minutes. Full-body mats are often used for 20-30 minutes, while localized devices may be used for shorter periods. Your device's guidelines will provide specific recommendations.

How often should I use PEMF therapy?

Frequency depends on your goals and the device. Some people use PEMF daily as part of their wellness routine, while others use it several times per week. Consult your device's guidelines and listen to your body. Consistency is often more important than frequency.

Usage Questions

Can I use PEMF therapy with other treatments?

PEMF therapy generally complements other wellness practices. However, always consult your healthcare provider about combining therapies, especially if you're under medical care or taking medications.

Are there any side effects?

Most people experience no negative effects from PEMF therapy. Some users report temporary tingling or warmth during sessions, which is normal. If you experience any discomfort, discontinue use and consult your healthcare provider.

Can I use PEMF if I have a pacemaker or implanted device?

No. People with pacemakers, insulin pumps, or other electronic implanted devices should not use PEMF therapy. Always consult your healthcare provider before starting PEMF if you have any medical conditions or concerns.

Can pregnant women use PEMF therapy?

We do not recommend PEMF therapy during pregnancy unless specifically approved by your healthcare provider. Always consult your doctor before using any wellness technology during pregnancy.
