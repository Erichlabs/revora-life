## What Does PEMF Stand For?

**PEMF = Pulsed Electromagnetic Field**

Let's break that down:
- **Pulsed** — Delivered in bursts or pulses, not continuously
- **Electromagnetic** — Uses magnetic fields (like the Earth produces)
- **Field** — An area of energy that surrounds us

In simple terms, PEMF therapy uses gentle electromagnetic pulses to support your body's natural functions at the cellular level.

Today's lifestyles expose us to factors that can disrupt natural cellular energy:
- Electronic devices and EMF exposure
- Chronic stress

Over time, these factors can leave your cells running on low battery.

### How PEMF Helps

PEMF therapy delivers gentle electromagnetic pulses that:
1. **Penetrate the body** — Reaching cells throughout your system

The frequencies used in PEMF therapy are low-intensity and similar to the natural electromagnetic frequencies produced by the Earth.

## Is PEMF Therapy Safe?

### Generally Recognized As Safe
PEMF therapy is generally considered safe for most people. The technology has been:
- Studied for decades
- Used in medical settings worldwide
- Cleared by regulatory bodies for wellness use

### Who Should Avoid PEMF?
You should NOT use PEMF therapy if you have:
- Pacemaker or implanted electronic device
- Insulin pump
- Other electronic implants
- Pregnancy (unless approved by your doctor)

### Always Consult Your Doctor
Before starting any new wellness practice, especially if you:
- Have a medical condition
- Are taking medications
- Are under medical care
- Have concerns about suitability
